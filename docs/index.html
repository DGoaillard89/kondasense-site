Why legionella

Temperature is your first line of defence

Legionella bacteria multiply in water between 20°C and 45°C. Under the HSE's Approved Code of Practice L8, duty holders must control that risk and keep records that prove it.

Most sites still rely on monthly manual checks, which give a snapshot once a month and leave weeks of blind spots in between. Konda Sence monitors continuously, so problems are caught in minutes, not weeks.

  • Supports ACoP L8 and HSG274 Part 2 temperature monitoring
  • Time-stamped evidence for audits and insurers
  • Fewer site visits and less manual testing

Little-used outlets

Only flush the outlets that need flushing

Under HSG274, outlets that aren't used regularly should be flushed at least weekly. On a large site that means someone walking every corridor and running every tap, which can add up to a full-time salary.

Konda Nodes detect each time water flows through an outlet. Each week you get a short list of the outlets that haven't been used, so you only flush those, and every flush is logged.
